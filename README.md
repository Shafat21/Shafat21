### 🚀 Featured project

<h3 align="center"><a href="https://github.com/Shafat21/DynamicNotch">🏝️ Dynamic Notch Island</a></h3>

<p align="center">
  A pill at the top of your screen that turns into a dashboard: music with synced lyrics, calendar, weather,
  notes, reminders, clipboard history, offline voice dictation, and <b>Pal</b>, a companion for coding agents
  (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Copilot CLI) that lets you approve prompts right from the island.
</p>
